# Opening the example

Extract the whole directory. Open `project.cnoid` in the Workcell local workspace. See the [project instructions](README.md) to select the saved tracks, move the time bar, and edit the cycle. No model request is required to open the scene.

A relocated copy reopened all six bodies and all three 1,791-frame motion tracks. At 32 selected times in the 35.8-second sequence, the native time bar reproduced the expected arm, finger, chuck, door and blank states. The blank ended in the finished nest at [0.303, 0.310, 0.180] metres. The saved tracks run at 50 Hz.

This is a scripted kinematic sequence, not material removal or measured grasp force. It does not certify industrial operation, guarding or continuous clearance. Original robot source and license: [attribution](ATTRIBUTION.md).
